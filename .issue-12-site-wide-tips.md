## Goal

The dashboard tip stays open on the wrong card. Hovering New clients and then clicking Revenue leaves Revenue's tip stuck. Clicking a card should open that card's tip and close any other open tip.

The same small tip belongs on the rest of the site, not only the four dashboard cards. It sits above an info mark. It does not open as a block inside the card, and it does not cover the number.

## Steps

- [ ] On the dashboard, keep one tip open at a time. Clicking a card opens its tip and closes the others. Clicking outside, or leaving the card, closes it.
- [ ] Keep the short line under the number and the info mark by the title. The tip stays the small one above the card.
- [ ] Use that same tip anywhere else on the site where a number or a label needs one sentence, on the office pages and the public pages. Skip headings that are already plain.

## Done when

- [ ] Hover New clients, then click Revenue. Revenue's tip is the one open. New clients' tip is closed. The number stays visible.
- [ ] Click outside. The tip closes. Hovering another card opens only that card's tip.
- [ ] The same small tip shows on the other pages that need one sentence, and the public homepage still reads the same where no tip was needed.

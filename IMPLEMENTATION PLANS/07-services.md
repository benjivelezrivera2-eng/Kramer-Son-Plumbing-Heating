## Where these prices live

They live in the services list kept in Supabase. Supabase is the online filing cabinet for the office.

Each service is one row in that list. The starting price sits on that same row, next to the name. This is the same idea as the lesson: each kind of service has its usual price saved with it.

Seven of these are plumbing: drain cleaning, sewer line repair, water heater repair, water heater installation, leak detection, toilet and fixture repair, and pipe and water line repair. Furnace and heating repair is the only one under heating. Water heater repair and water heater installation stay with plumbing, because they are water heaters, not the furnace.

The public website does not pick up these numbers in this step.

## What changes on the office Services screen

The Services page in the office will show each of these eight services with its starting price beside the name.

Today that page is only a sample. These eight prices show on that sample for the office. They are not saved in Supabase yet.

Each number below is a starting point for the office, not the bill the customer pays. Someone still looks at the job first. The price can go up or down after that look, the same way the shop already promises a price before work starts. If a number is wrong, say the one you want instead.

## Drain cleaning

Issue [#33](https://github.com/benjivelezrivera2-eng/Kramer-Son-Plumbing-Heating/issues/33).

Drain cleaning means clearing a clog so a sink, tub, or main line flows again.

The suggested starting price is **$149**.

## Water heater repair

Issue [#34](https://github.com/benjivelezrivera2-eng/Kramer-Son-Plumbing-Heating/issues/34).

Water heater repair means looking at a tank or a tankless unit and fixing it on that visit when it can be done.

The suggested starting price is **$189**.

This is repair, not a brand-new heater. Water heater installation stays a separate service and does not get this price.

## Leak detection

Issue [#35](https://github.com/benjivelezrivera2-eng/Kramer-Son-Plumbing-Heating/issues/35).

Leak detection means finding a hidden leak before it ruins floors, walls, or the water bill.

The suggested starting price is **$249**.

That number is a starting point for finding the leak, not the cost of the repair after it is found. Pipe and water line repair is a separate service with its own starting price.

## Toilet repairs

Issue [#36](https://github.com/benjivelezrivera2-eng/Kramer-Son-Plumbing-Heating/issues/36).

Toilet repairs means a running toilet, a dripping faucet, or a simple fixture fix, done cleanly.

On the services list this service is named Toilet and Fixture Repair.

The suggested starting price is **$129**.

## Furnace repairs

Issue [#37](https://github.com/benjivelezrivera2-eng/Kramer-Son-Plumbing-Heating/issues/37).

Furnace repairs means getting the heat back on, checking the system, and saying what the repair will take.

On the services list this service is named Furnace and Heating Repair.

The suggested starting price is **$189**.

This is the only one of the eight that belongs with heating.

## Pipe and water line

Issue [#38](https://github.com/benjivelezrivera2-eng/Kramer-Son-Plumbing-Heating/issues/38).

Pipe and water line repair means stopping a leak, replacing the bad section, and checking that the line holds before anyone leaves.

On the services list this service is named Pipe and Water Line Repair.

The suggested starting price is **$275**.

That number is a starting point for the repair itself. Finding a hidden leak is the separate leak-detection service, with its own starting price.

## Sewer line repair

Sewer line repair means finding the break in the line, repairing that section, and keeping wastewater where it belongs.

On the services list this service is named Sewer Line Repair.

The suggested starting price is **$425**.

Drain cleaning at $149 is clearing a clog so the line flows again. Sewer line repair is the line itself, so the start sits above pipe and water line repair at $275. Someone still looks at the job first. The price can go up after that look.

## Water heater installation

Water heater installation means sizing a replacement to the house and getting hot water back on the same day.

On the services list this service is named Water Heater Installation.

The suggested starting price is **$399**.

Water heater repair at $189 is a fix on the heater that is already there. Installation is a new heater, so it does not use the repair price. The number is a starting point for the visit. The heater itself can change the bill after someone looks at the house.
